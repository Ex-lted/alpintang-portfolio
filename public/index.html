Chrome extension · Solo project

CLiCScrape

A Chrome extension that walks you through MMU’s enrollment pages step by step, retrieves the class options for the courses you pick from pages you open yourself, and generates timetables that fit around every one of them.

Filter by free days, earliest start, latest end, or open classes only, then compare each plan as a weekly grid. Everything runs in your browser, with no server and no accounts.
